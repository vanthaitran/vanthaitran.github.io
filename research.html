Polymer melt pool crystallization measurements

An image-based way to monitor how polymer melt pools crystallize in powder bed fusion, such as Multi Jet Fusion. As crystals grow they scatter more light and the melt pool darkens; tracking brightness in captured images, region by region, gives the time to equilibrium, crystal growth rate and spherulite size. The measurements can feed back to the printer to adjust the cooling profile toward a crystallization target.

Inkjet-printed ZnO UV photodetectors

A fully inkjet-printed ultraviolet photodetector fabricated on a flexible substrate, demonstrating additive manufacturing of inorganic semiconductor devices for wearable optoelectronics, and a study showing that low-temperature annealing raises the printed films’ responsivity by more than a thousand times.
