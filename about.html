About

I work at the intersection of robotics and additive manufacturing, focusing on robotic systems, sensing, and control methods for precise fabrication, inspection, and repair in real-world environments.

I completed my PhD at Nanyang Technological University in 2019, with a thesis on inkjet-printed ZnO thin-film semiconductors for additive manufacturing of electronic devices. My publications span printed and flexible electronics, UV photodetectors, wearable sensors, acoustofluidics and the modelling of laser powder bed fusion.
