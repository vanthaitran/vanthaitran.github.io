About

I work at the intersection of robotics and additive manufacturing, focusing on robotic systems, sensing, and control methods for precise fabrication, inspection, and repair in real-world environments. I am based at the Singapore Centre for 3D Printing (SC3DP) and the School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering (MAE), Nanyang Technological University.

I completed my PhD at Nanyang Technological University in 2019, with a thesis on inkjet-printed ZnO thin-film semiconductors for additive manufacturing of electronic devices. My publications span printed and flexible electronics, UV photodetectors, wearable sensors, acoustofluidics and the modelling of laser powder bed fusion. In 2024 I returned to robotics, which is now the focus of my work.
